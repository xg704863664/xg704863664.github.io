How Long Does Rice Last?

The difference between white and brown rice is enormous: decades versus months. Here are the real numbers and how to store each.

Storage times

FormPantrySealed / mylarFreezer
White rice2 years25–30 yearsIndefinite
Brown rice3–6 months6–12 months18 months
Cooked rice (fridge)3–4 days——
Cooked rice (frozen)——6 months
Wild rice6–12 months2 years2 years

Why brown rice spoils so much faster

The bran layer contains oils. Those oils oxidise and go rancid — the rice smells like old paint or crayons and tastes bitter. White rice has the bran removed, which is exactly why it stores for decades.

If you keep brown rice more than a few months, freeze it.

The real danger: cooked rice

Uncooked rice is safe. Cooked rice is the risky one. It can carry Bacillus cereus spores that survive cooking and multiply at room temperature.

  • Cool cooked rice within 1 hour and refrigerate immediately
  • Do not leave it at room temperature more than 2 hours
  • Reheat until steaming hot, and only reheat once
  • Fridge life: 3–4 days maximum

"Fried rice syndrome" is real and unpleasant. When in doubt, throw it out.

How to store rice properly

  1. Oxygen absorbers — 300 cc per 4 litres of rice for long-term storage
  2. Mylar bags or food-grade buckets — the standard for decades-long storage
  3. Cool, dark, dry — heat and light accelerate rancidity in brown rice
  4. Bay leaves — traditional against weevils, though not reliable on their own
  5. Freeze 48 hours on arrival — kills any eggs already in the bag

Signs rice has gone bad

  • Smell — rancid, paint-like or musty
  • Colour — yellowing or dark spots
  • Insects — weevils, or fine dust in the bag
  • Taste — bitter or sour after cooking

Mouldy rice should always be discarded — some moulds produce toxins that cooking does not destroy.

Common questions

Can you eat rice after 5 years?

White rice stored properly in mylar with oxygen absorbers, yes. Brown rice, no — the oils will have gone rancid.

How long does cooked rice last in the fridge?

3-4 days if cooled within an hour of cooking and refrigerated immediately. Reheat only once, until steaming hot.

Does rice go bad in the pantry?

White rice keeps about 2 years in a normal pantry. Brown rice only 3-6 months because the bran oils go rancid.

Is it safe to eat rice left out overnight?

No. Bacillus cereus spores multiply at room temperature and reheating does not destroy the toxin. Discard rice left out more than 2 hours.
