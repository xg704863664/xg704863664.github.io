Why Do Chickens Eat Their Eggs?

Egg eating almost always starts with an accident: an egg breaks, a hen tastes it, and the behaviour spreads through the flock within days. It is one of the hardest habits to break, so act immediately.

The fixes, in order of effectiveness

1. Collect eggs twice a day

The single most effective change. An egg left in the nest is an egg that gets broken, and a broken egg teaches the whole flock.

2. Fix the shells first

Thin shells break, and broken eggs get eaten. Offer oyster shell free-choice in a separate dish from the feed — hens take what they need.

Do not rely on crushed eggshell alone; it is a fraction of the calcium in oyster shell and it teaches the taste.

3. Roll-away nest boxes

These slope so the egg rolls out of reach as soon as it is laid. If the habit is established, this is the most reliable fix — the hen simply cannot get to the egg.

4. Darken the nest boxes

Hens look for a dark, enclosed place. Bright nest boxes make eggs more visible and more interesting.

5. Break the habit with a decoy

Blow out a raw egg, fill it with mustard or a little dish soap, and leave it in the nest. The hen pecks, gets an unpleasant mouthful, and usually stops.

This works best early, before the behaviour is established across the flock.

6. Add space and clutter

Under 2 sq ft per bird inside the coop, or a bare run with nothing to do, and pecking behaviours start. Add pallets, branches, a hanging cabbage.

How long does it take to stop

If you catch it in the first week and fix the underlying cause, most flocks stop within a few days. If it has been going on for weeks, expect to need roll-away boxes permanently.

A hen that has eaten eggs for months rarely stops even with every fix applied. In that case, culling is the honest answer — the alternative is losing most of your eggs.

Do not do these

  • Do not leave eggs as decoys hoping they will get bored. You are training them.
  • Do not feed eggshells back if you already have an egg-eating problem — it reinforces the taste.
  • Do not add a mustard egg and leave it. Replace it after a day; a broken decoy becomes a real egg to eat.

Common questions

Why do chickens eat their own eggs?

Usually an accident: a thin-shelled egg breaks, a hen tastes it, and the behaviour spreads. Other causes are crowding, boredom, protein deficiency and bright nest boxes.

How do you stop chickens eating eggs?

Collect eggs twice a day, offer oyster shell free-choice to fix thin shells, darken and lower the nest boxes, and add clutter. For an established habit, roll-away nest boxes are the most reliable fix.

Does a mustard egg stop egg eating?

Often, if used early. Blow out an egg, fill it with mustard, and leave it in the nest. The hen pecks once and usually stops. It works poorly once the whole flock has the habit.

Will chickens stop eating eggs on their own?

Rarely. The behaviour is self-rewarding and spreads. Fix the underlying cause immediately or it becomes permanent in the flock.
