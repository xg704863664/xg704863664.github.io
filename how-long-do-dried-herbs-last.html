How Long Do Dried Herbs Last?

Dried herbs do not go mouldy so much as go quiet. They look fine and taste of dust. Most lose real potency within 6–12 months.

Storage times

Herb typeWholeGround / crushed
Leafy herbs (basil, oregano, thyme)1–2 years6–12 months
Woody herbs (rosemary, sage)2–3 years1–2 years
Seeds (fennel, cumin, coriander)3–4 years1–2 years
Roots (ginger, turmeric, liquorice)2–3 years1–2 years
Flowers (chamomile, lavender)1–2 years—
Home-dried herbs6–12 months3–6 months

Whole keeps far longer than ground. Grinding exposes far more surface area to air. Buy whole seeds and crush them when you cook — the difference is not subtle.

The crumble test

Take a pinch and rub it between your fingers:

  • Crumples and releases a strong smell — still good
  • Crumples but smells faint — weak; use double the amount
  • Feels like dust, or will not crumble at all — dead. Compost it.

Colour is a guide too: good dried oregano is green-grey. If it is uniformly brown, the oils have oxidised.

How to store them properly

  1. Airtight glass, not plastic bags — the bags they come in are the worst option
  2. Dark — light destroys the aromatic oils. A cupboard, not a windowsill rack.
  3. Cool — above the stove is the worst place in the kitchen
  4. Whole, not ground — crush at the moment of use
  5. Label with the date — you will not remember

Why home-dried herbs fade faster

Commercial dried herbs are often dried fast at controlled temperature, which preserves more oil. Air-dried at home they keep less well — especially if they were not fully dry when stored.

An incompletely dried herb does not just lose potency; it can grow mould in the jar. See our guide to drying herbs for the test: a leaf should snap, not bend.

Do dried herbs go bad?

They rarely become unsafe, with two exceptions:

  • Mould — visible fuzz or a musty smell. Discard.
  • Pests — webbing or fine dust in the jar. Discard.

Otherwise an old herb is not dangerous, just pointless. You are adding colour, not flavour.

Reviving the flavour of old herbs

You cannot restore lost oils, but two things help:

  • Bloom them in oil or fat — heat releases what is left far more effectively than adding to water
  • Use more — up to double, but taste as you go
  • Add fresh at the end — a little fresh herb plus old dried herb works better than either alone

Common questions

How long do dried herbs keep their flavour?

Whole leafy herbs about 1-2 years, ground spices 6-12 months, and whole seeds 3-4 years. Home-dried herbs fade faster, usually within a year.

How do you know if dried herbs are still good?

Rub a pinch between your fingers. If it crumbles and smells strong it is fine; if it feels like dust or smells faint, it is spent.

Do dried herbs expire?

They rarely become unsafe, but they lose potency. Discard if there is mould, webbing or a musty smell.

Should dried herbs be stored in the fridge?

No. A cool, dark, dry cupboard is better. Fridge humidity causes clumping and introduces moisture each time you open the jar.
