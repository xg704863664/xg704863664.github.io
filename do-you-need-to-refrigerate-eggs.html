Do You Need to Refrigerate Eggs?

It depends entirely on where you live — and the reason is not climate, it is whether the eggs were washed.

The short answer

WhereRefrigerate?WhyShelf life
USA, Canada, Japan, AustraliaYesEggs are washed, which removes the protective bloom3–5 weeks
UK, most of EuropeNoEggs are unwashed and keep their bloom3 weeks at room temp
Your own hensNo, if unwashedYou have not stripped the bloom2–3 weeks on the counter

Why washing changes everything

A freshly laid egg has a thin protein coating called the bloom (or cuticle). It seals the shell's pores and blocks bacteria from getting in.

Commercial washing removes the bloom along with the dirt. Once it is gone, bacteria can cross the shell — so the eggs must be refrigerated and kept cold continuously.

That is why US supermarkets sell refrigerated eggs, and why European supermarkets sell them on an ordinary shelf.

The temperature shock problem

If eggs have been refrigerated, keep them refrigerated. Moving cold eggs to a warm counter causes condensation on the shell, and that moisture helps bacteria move through the pores.

Conversely, unwashed eggs kept at room temperature should not be put in the fridge later — the same condensation happens.

Pick one and stay consistent.

How long do eggs actually last

  • Refrigerated, washed: 3–5 weeks from purchase
  • Room temperature, unwashed: 2–3 weeks
  • Frozen (out of shell): up to 12 months
  • Hard boiled, fridge: 1 week

These are quality limits, not safety limits — properly handled eggs rarely spoil in that window.

How to test whether an egg is still good

The float test. Put the egg in a bowl of water:

  • Sinks and lies flat — fresh
  • Sinks but stands upright — a few weeks old, still fine to eat
  • Floats — the air pocket has grown too large; discard

The egg does not float because it has gone off — it floats because water has evaporated through the shell and been replaced by air. A floating egg is old, and old eggs are more likely to harbour bacteria.

Should you wash your own eggs?

No — not if you want to store them at room temperature. Wiping with a dry cloth or a little fine sandpaper is enough for a dirty egg.

If you do wash them, refrigerate them immediately and treat them as washed eggs from then on.

Do not store eggs in the fridge door

The door is the warmest part of the fridge and the temperature swings every time it opens. Keep eggs in their box on a middle shelf.

Keeping them in the original carton also stops them absorbing fridge odours through the porous shell.

Common questions

Do you need to refrigerate eggs?

In the US, Canada, Japan and Australia, yes - commercial eggs are washed, which removes the protective bloom. In the UK and most of Europe, unwashed eggs keep for about 3 weeks at room temperature.

How long do eggs last without refrigeration?

About 2-3 weeks for unwashed eggs at room temperature. Washed eggs must be refrigerated and last 3-5 weeks.

Why do Americans refrigerate eggs but Europeans do not?

Because US eggs are washed before sale, which removes the bloom - the natural coating that seals the shell. European eggs are left unwashed and keep their protection.

How do you tell if an egg has gone bad?

Put it in a bowl of water. Fresh eggs sink and lie flat; older ones stand upright; floating eggs should be discarded. Also trust your nose - a bad egg smells unmistakable when cracked.
