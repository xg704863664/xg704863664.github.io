The three rules behind all of it

  1. Oils decide shelf life. Anything with the bran or germ intact — brown rice, whole wheat flour, nuts, whole oats — goes rancid in months. Remove the oils (white rice, white flour) and it keeps for decades.
  2. Moisture and oxygen are the enemies. Airtight with an oxygen absorber is the difference between 2 years and 25.
  3. Heat accelerates everything. A pantry at 30°C halves the life of almost everything on this list compared with 15°C.

Signs food has actually gone bad

  • Rancid — smells like old paint or crayons. Common in whole grains, nuts and old oil.
  • Mould — discard. Mycotoxins survive cooking.
  • Bulging or dented cans — discard without opening. Botulism risk.
  • Off smell when opened — trust it, even if the date says otherwise.

Note the difference: stale is a texture problem and usually safe; rancid and mouldy are not.

Common questions

How long does rice last?

White rice keeps about 2 years in a pantry and 25-30 years sealed with oxygen absorbers. Brown rice only 3-6 months because the bran oils go rancid.

Does honey really never expire?

Correct - sealed honey is effectively indefinite. Crystallisation is not spoilage; warm the jar gently to liquefy it.

How long do eggs last?

Unwashed eggs keep 2-3 weeks at room temperature, or 3-5 months refrigerated. Washing removes the protective bloom and shortens that dramatically.

Can you eat food past its best-before date?

Usually yes for dry goods. Best-before is a quality guide, not a safety limit. Use-by dates on perishables are different and should be respected.
