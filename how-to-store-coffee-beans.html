How to Store Coffee Beans

Coffee starts losing flavour within days of roasting. Four things destroy it: oxygen, light, heat and moisture. Almost every storage mistake is one of those four.

Storage times by method

MethodWhole beansGround
Airtight, dark, room temp2–4 weeks3–5 days
Original bag, rolled shut1–2 weeks2–3 days

Why not the fridge

The fridge is cold, which is good, but it is also humid and full of strong smells.

  • Condensation: every time you take the beans out, moisture condenses on them. Water is the fastest way to stale coffee.
  • Odour transfer: coffee beans are porous and absorb whatever is nearby — yesterday's leftovers included.

The freezer avoids both problems if you seal the beans properly and do not open the container repeatedly.

The four enemies

Whole beans or ground?

Whole beans, always, if you care about flavour. Grinding exposes a vastly larger surface area to oxygen.

Ground coffee loses most of its aroma within a few days. Whole beans keep 2–4 weeks at room temperature. That is why the standard advice is to grind immediately before brewing.

How to freeze coffee properly

Freezing works well, but only if you do it right:

  1. Divide into single-use portions — a week's worth per bag. This is the key step; repeatedly opening one big bag defeats the purpose.
  2. Remove as much air as possible and seal. Vacuum sealing is ideal; a zip bag with the air pressed out is fine.
  3. Freeze immediately after roasting — do not wait two weeks and then freeze stale beans.
  4. Grind from frozen. You do not need to thaw the beans; frozen beans grind well and thaw instantly.
  5. Never refreeze. Once a portion is out, use it.

What about the one-way valve bag?

Good coffee bags have a one-way valve that lets CO₂ out but keeps oxygen from getting in. They are designed for storage — but only until you open them.

Once opened, roll the bag down, clip it, and use it within two weeks. Or transfer to a smaller airtight container so there is less air around the beans.

Signs your coffee has gone stale

  • No aroma when you open the container
  • The beans look dull rather than oily
  • The brewed cup tastes flat, papery or cardboard-like
  • No "bloom" — the puffed-up crust — when you pour hot water on

Common questions

How long do coffee beans last?

Whole beans keep 2-4 weeks in an airtight dark container at room temperature, or 3-6 months in the freezer in sealed portions. Ground coffee lasts only 3-5 days at room temperature.

Should coffee beans be stored in the fridge?

No. The fridge is humid and full of odours. Coffee absorbs moisture and smells, and condensation forms every time you take the container out. Use the freezer instead, in sealed single-use portions.

Can you freeze coffee beans?

Yes - 3-6 months. Divide into single-use portions, remove the air and seal. Grind straight from frozen; there is no need to thaw.

How do you know if coffee beans are stale?

No aroma when opened, dull rather than oily beans, a flat or cardboard taste in the cup, and no bloom when hot water hits the grounds.
