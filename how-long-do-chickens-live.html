How Long Do Chickens Live?

A backyard hen usually lives 6 to 10 years, but she only lays well for the first 2 to 3. Here is what to expect, breed by breed, and what actually cuts a life short.

Lifespan by type

TypeTypical lifespanLaying years
Hybrid layers (ISA Brown, sex-links)4–7 years2–3
Dual-purpose (Orpington, Sussex, Wyandotte)6–10 years3–5
Heritage breeds (Brahma, Australorp)8–12 years4–6
Bantams5–8 years3–4

Hybrid layers are bred to convert feed into eggs as fast as possible. That intensity is why they burn out sooner — it is a trade-off built into the bird.

How laying changes with age

  • Year 1: peak production. A good hybrid gives 280–320 eggs.
  • Year 2: down roughly 15–20%. Eggs get larger, which many people prefer.
  • Year 3: down another 15–20%, plus longer winter pauses.
  • Year 4+: a handful of eggs in spring, or none. She still eats the same feed.

This is the point most keepers have to decide something: an older hen costs about the same to feed as a productive one. Many keep them as pets, add pullets, or cull. There is no wrong answer, but it is worth deciding before year four rather than during it.

What shortens a hen's life

Predators

By far the biggest cause of early death. Raccoons, foxes, hawks and dogs. Hardware cloth, not chicken wire, and a roof on the run.

Reproductive problems

Egg binding, prolapse and internal laying are common in heavy-laying hybrids. A hen that strains for hours, or sits puffed up and quiet, needs attention the same day.

Respiratory disease

Damp, unventilated coops cause mycoplasma and other chronic infections. Ventilation matters more than warmth — ammonia build-up is the real enemy.

Heat, not cold

Chickens handle freezing temperatures far better than heat above 35°C / 95°F. Shade and cool water in summer save more birds than a heater in winter.

How to tell age without a hatch date

  • Legs and feet: scales get rougher and more raised with age
  • Comb: smaller and paler in older hens, especially out of season
  • Vent: wide and moist in a laying hen, small and dry in an old one
  • Eggs: larger and thinner-shelled as she ages

Common questions

How long do chickens live as pets?

With predator-proof housing and good feed, 8–10 years is realistic for dual-purpose breeds. The record for a hen is over 20.

Do hens stop laying if you leave the eggs?

Some go broody and stop for 3 weeks. Others carry on. Leaving eggs also risks a hidden nest building up.

Is it worth keeping a hen that stopped laying?

If she is a pet, yes. If the flock is for eggs, most keepers add pullets instead — a 4-year-old hen eats about the same as a 1-year-old.
