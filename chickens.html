Chicken Coop Size: How Much Space Per Bird

Getting the coop size wrong is the most expensive beginner mistake with chickens - not because the coop costs more, but because a crowded flock starts pecking, and that is hard to undo.

The two numbers that matter

AreaSpace per birdWhy
Inside the coop2-3 sq ftRoosting and shelter; less than this and bullying starts
In the run8-10 sq ftExercise and foraging; confinement without space causes feather-pulling

Bantams manage with about two-thirds of that. Large breeds - Orpingtons, Brahmas - need the upper end or more.

The run figures assume the birds are shut in. If they free-range for part of the day, the run can be smaller - but not the coop.

How to work it out for your flock

  • Multiply your number of birds by 2.5 for a mid-range coop size
  • Multiply by 9 for the run
  • Add nesting boxes at roughly one per 3-4 hens - they will all queue for the same one regardless
  • Add height: 45-60cm of headroom above the highest perch, or they cannot stand properly

Ventilation matters more than warmth

The instinct is to seal a coop against the cold. That is the wrong instinct. A sealed coop holds ammonia and moisture, and that causes respiratory disease far more often than cold does.

  • Vents above head height, ideally on two sides for cross-flow
  • Leave them open all winter - chickens handle cold far better than damp
  • Block direct draughts at bird level, never the high vents
  • If the coop smells sharp when you open it, ventilation is inadequate

Perches

  • Allow 20-25cm of perch per bird
  • Flat, wide perches (5cm+) are better than round dowel - chickens roost flat-footed
  • Higher perches for larger birds; bantams can use lower ones

Common sizing mistakes

  • Believing the label. Many coops sold as "for 6 hens" are built to the minimum for 3
  • Forgetting the run. A small coop with no run space is a cage
  • Buying for the birds you have rather than the birds you will have - chicken maths is real, and you will end up with more
  • Underestimating bedding volume. Bigger coops need more bedding, more often

Common questions

How much space does one chicken need in a coop?

2-3 square feet per bird inside, plus 8-10 square feet per bird in the run.

What size coop for 6 chickens?

About 12-18 square feet inside (roughly 3ft x 5ft) and a run of 50-60 square feet, plus nest boxes.

How many nest boxes for 6 hens?

Two. One per 3-4 hens - though they will all use the same one anyway.

Does a chicken coop need ventilation?

Yes, more than it needs insulation. Leave high vents open year-round to clear ammonia and moisture.
