Food Storage Chart: How Long Staples Actually Keep

Most food storage advice is either vague or wildly optimistic. This is a working chart - how long things genuinely keep, and how to tell when they have gone past it.

Pantry (cool, dark, dry)

ItemKeepsNotes
White rice2 years+Safe much longer; flavour fades
Brown rice6 monthsThe oils go rancid - smell it before cooking
White flour6-12 monthsLonger in the freezer
Wholemeal flour3 monthsRancid quickly; buy smaller amounts
Dried beans and lentils2-3 yearsOlder beans take much longer to soften
Pasta (dried)2 yearsNo real safety issue, texture declines
Oats12 monthsWatch for a stale, soapy smell
Sugar, saltIndefinitelyHarden rather than spoil
HoneyIndefinitelyCrystallises - warm it gently to restore
Olive and vegetable oil12-18 monthsRancid oil smells like crayons
Whole spices3-4 yearsGround spices only 6-12 months
Dried herbs12 monthsSafe after, but increasingly tasteless
Potatoes, onions1-3 monthsDark, cool, ventilated; keep them apart - each makes the other sprout

Fridge

ItemKeepsNotes
Eggs3-5 weeksFloat test: fresh sinks, bad floats
Milk5-7 days openedFreezes well, but expands - leave headroom
Hard cheese3-4 weeksCut mould off with 2cm margin
Soft cheese1-2 weeksDiscard if moulded - it spreads through
Butter1-3 monthsFreezes for 6+ months
Cooked leftovers3-4 daysCool fast, refrigerate within 2 hours
Leafy greens3-5 daysDamp paper towel in a bag extends it
Root vegetables2-4 weeksRemove tops - they draw moisture

Freezer (at -18C / 0F)

ItemKeepsNotes
Beef, lamb6-12 monthsFreezer burn is quality, not safety
Pork, poultry6-9 monthsWrap tightly; air is the enemy
Fish3-6 monthsOily fish shorter - 3 months
Blanched vegetables8-12 monthsBlanching first preserves colour and vitamins
Fruit8-12 monthsFreeze on a tray first so it does not clump
Bread3 monthsSlice before freezing
Cooked meals2-3 monthsLabel with the date - you will not remember

Canned and jarred

  • Low-acid (beans, meat, soup): 2-5 years
  • High-acid (tomatoes, fruit, pickles): 12-18 months for best quality
  • Home-canned: use within a year, and follow tested processing times - this is the one area where guesswork is genuinely dangerous

Discard any can that is

  • Bulging or dented on a seam
  • Leaking, or sticky on the outside
  • Smells wrong when opened, or fizzes

The five rules that matter more than the chart

  • Cool, dark, dry. Heat and light shorten almost everything
  • Airtight. Oxygen and moisture do most of the damage
  • Label the date. You will not remember, and neither will the jar
  • Rotate. New stock at the back, old at the front
  • Buy what you eat. A full pantry of food you do not cook is just a slow loss

Signs it has actually gone off

  • Rancid: a crayon, paint or soapy smell - oils and whole grains
  • Mould: discard soft foods entirely; hard foods can be cut with a wide margin
  • Off smells or fizzing: discard, do not taste to check
  • Freezer burn: greyish dry patches - safe but unpleasant; cut them off

Common questions

How long does white rice keep?

2 years before quality drops. Brown rice is very different - the oils go rancid in about 6 months.

How long can you store flour?

White flour 6-12 months in the pantry. Wholemeal about 3 months, or freeze it.

How long do canned goods last?

2-5 years for low-acid foods, 12-18 months for high-acid. Discard bulging, leaking or seam-dented cans.

How long do eggs keep?

3-5 weeks refrigerated. The float test is the practical check: fresh eggs sink.
