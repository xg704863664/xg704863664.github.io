How Long Does Flour Last?

The difference between white and whole wheat flour is the same as rice: years versus months. The bran oils are the reason.

Why whole wheat spoils faster

Whole wheat keeps the bran and germ, which contain oils. Those oils oxidise and go rancid — the flour smells like old paint and tastes bitter. White flour has them removed, so it stores for years.

Practical rule: buy whole wheat in small amounts, or freeze it.

How to store flour

  • Airtight container — not the paper bag it came in
  • Cool and dark — heat accelerates rancidity
  • Freeze for long storage — bring to room temperature before baking so it does not shock the yeast
  • Bay leaves — traditional deterrent, but not reliable alone
  • Freeze 48 hours on arrival — kills any weevil eggs already present

Weevils: what to do

Finding small brown beetles in flour is unpleasant but not dangerous — they are not disease carriers. Options:

  1. Sift and use immediately for baking; the flour is still safe
  2. Freeze the flour for 4 days to kill eggs and larvae, then sift
  3. Discard if there is webbing or mould — that is a different problem

Prevention beats cure: store in airtight containers and freeze new bags for 48 hours.

Signs flour has gone bad

  • Smell — sour, musty or paint-like (rancid)
  • Colour — greyish or discoloured
  • Texture — clumping that will not break up
  • Taste — bitter, especially whole wheat

Mouldy flour should always be thrown out — mycotoxins are not destroyed by baking.

Does flour really expire?

White flour past its date is usually fine if stored well — the date is a quality guide, not a safety limit. Whole wheat flour is the opposite: it genuinely degrades, and rancid flour makes bitter baked goods.

Common questions

How long does flour last past its expiration date?

White flour stored airtight in a cool place can be good for a year or more past the date. Whole wheat flour should be used within months regardless.

Can you freeze flour?

Yes, and it is the best long-term option. Freeze in airtight bags, and let it come to room temperature before baking.

Are weevils in flour dangerous?

No. They are not disease carriers. Sift them out or freeze the flour for four days, then use it.

Why did my whole wheat flour go bitter?

The bran oils went rancid. Store whole wheat in the fridge or freezer if you keep it more than a few months.
